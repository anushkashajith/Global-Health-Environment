# Global Healthcare & Socioeconomic Analysis

# Project Overview

This project explores global healthcare, socioeconomic, and environmental indicators using an interactive **Power BI dashboard**. The analysis focuses on understanding how factors such as healthcare infrastructure, economic conditions, pollution, and lifestyle behaviors influence health outcomes across different countries.

The dashboard enables users to explore relationships between healthcare access, mortality rates, economic indicators, and lifestyle risk factors using interactive visualizations and filters.

---

# Project Objectives

The goal of this project is to analyze global health trends and answer key questions such as:

* How does **life expectancy vary across countries and over time**?
* What relationship exists between **healthcare infrastructure and health outcomes**?
* Does **tobacco prevalence influence cardiovascular deaths**?
* How do **economic indicators like GDP per capita** correlate with life expectancy?
* How does **universal healthcare coverage impact mortality rates**?

---

#  Dashboard Features

##  Global Health Overview

Provides a high-level summary of health outcomes worldwide.

Visualizations include:

* Life expectancy trends over time
* Country comparison of life expectancy
* Global health indicators overview

---

##  Healthcare Infrastructure Analysis

Examines how healthcare access impacts health outcomes.

Key visualizations:

* **Doctors vs Life Expectancy** (Scatter Plot)
* **Health Expenditure vs Infant Mortality**
* **Universal Healthcare Coverage comparison by country**

---

##  Lifestyle Risk Factor Analysis

Analyzes how lifestyle behaviors affect mortality.

Example visualizations:

* **Tobacco Prevalence vs Cardiovascular Death Percentage**
* Lifestyle risk comparisons across countries

---

##  Environmental Health Analysis

Evaluates the impact of environmental conditions on public health.

Visualizations include:

* Air pollution–related death rates
* Environmental risk factor comparisons across countries

---

# Dashboard Interactivity

The dashboard allows users to explore the data using interactive filters:

* **Country**
* **Year**
* **Gender**

Users can dynamically compare health outcomes across regions and time periods.

---

# Key Insights (Example)

Some key observations from the dashboard include:

* Countries with **higher doctor availability tend to have higher life expectancy**.
* **Higher tobacco prevalence is associated with increased cardiovascular deaths**.
* **Greater healthcare spending is linked to lower infant mortality rates**.
* Countries with **higher universal healthcare coverage show improved overall health outcomes**.
